Keynotes

Our 2021 keynotes are Professor Susan Halford and Rakhi Rajani.

Susan is the founding co-Director of the Bristol Digital Futures Institute (BDFI), which is at the centre of the University’s new Temple Quarter innovation campus. BDFI is a University Research Institute that pioneers transformative approaches to digital innovation.

Rakhi is Chief Digital Officer at Genomics England where she leads interdisciplinary teams in developing complex healthcare services. She has always sat at the intersection of psychology, emerging technology and design and has worked across a range of industries to help organisations experiment, think forward and invent the future.
