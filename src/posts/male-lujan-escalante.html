Dr Malé Luján Escalante

Lecturer in Design-led Innovation, The Centre for Innovation and Entrepreneurship, University of Bristol

Malé is the Co-Director of IsITethical? Exchange, a service and community platform working with tech-industries in the medical, disaster response and risk management domains. She uses creative practice to design spaces for knowledge exchange and catalyse cross-disciplinary, cross-sector collaboration. She likes to play, mementos and magic tokens.

malelujanescalante.com

Dancing With The Trouble Of AI

Malé and her co-facilitators are leading a workshop on Friday 21 May. They are responsible innovation creative practitioners from IsITethical? Exchange, working with performance art, aikido, sci-fi and street dance to choreograph a ritual in which each movement embodies an ethical principle for innovating in AI.
