We are delighted to announce Spotify as our Gold Partner at Anthropology + Technology 2020.

“We’re excited to be the Gold Partner of Anthropology + Technology 2020. Like A+T, we are invested in socially-responsible AI and we welcome the opportunity to support an organisation which is exploring the important and complex landscape of emerging technologies and how they affect the world we live in.”

Amy Walker, Global Head of Insights Operations, Spotify

The two-day Anthropology + Technology Conference champions socially-responsible artificial intelligence (AI) and brings together speakers from the social sciences, technology, business, and the public sector. Delegates will explore the social and economic impact of AI-technology across fintech, health tech and smart cities, examining how AI can be built, designed, and deployed responsibly, without putting a brake on innovation.

Keynote speaker Nani Jansen Reventlow, a human rights lawyer and director of the Digital Freedom Fund, will address dismantling the systems of racism and colonisation embedded in digital technologies and how we can create a better future for everyone.

We hope you can join us at this unique two-day conference, now online and in its second year, which is taking place on Friday 9 October and Monday 12 October. We’re really excited about the conversations we’ll be having – together – on how to build and design emerging technologies in a socially-responsible way.

Thank you Spotify for your support.
