Chief Technology Officer, Microsoft Consulting Services UK

Richard is the Chief Technology Officer, Microsoft Consulting Services UK.

Richard advises business leaders around the world on a range of digital transformation challenges. However, it’s the building of innovative enterprises that is his real passion. In his day job Richard works with Microsoft’s customers giving them clarity and confidence about their digital journeys, helping them turn their aspirations into transformational business results. He is also the AI Ethics Lead for Microsoft UK.

Richard will officially open the 2020 edition of the Anthropology + Technology Conference and will be participating in the Smart Cities stream throughout the day.
