In this episode, we are in conversation with Anders Kofod-Petersen, who is talking in the FinTech stream at the conference in October.

Anders is the Deputy Director of the Alexandra Institute in Copenhagen and also a Professor of Artificial Intelligence at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology.

Anders shares his favourite success story, the Danish Natural Language Processing repository, and why collaboration is essential, both across companies and disciplines. He shares his beliefs on why we need social scientists to help build technology. And why an AI project should be seen as organisational one rather than an IT project.

We also discuss responsible AI and the ways in which companies can be encouraged to build AI responsibly without involving regulation.

Lastly, Anders offers a challenge to anthropologists and other social scientists.
