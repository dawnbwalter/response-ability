Watch Coded Bias with us

As a relaxing finish to the 2021 Summit, we’ll sink into our respective sofas at home with a glass of our favourite beverage and enjoy a Coded Bias Netflix Watch Party together.

About Coded Bias

Released globally on Netflix on April 5, 2021, Coded Bias [trailer] is an exploration into the fallout of MIT Media Lab researcher Joy Buolamwini’s discovery of racial bias in facial recognition algorithms.

What Coded Bias highlights best is that this battle to protect our rights as digital users in the 21st century cannot be won alone.

This affects everyone, and our Netflix Watch Party will equip you with all of the essential information to be the catalyst for change. Data rights are the unfinished business of the civil rights movement. Join us in the movement.

Download the official Coded Bias Discussion Guide [PDF, 40 pages, 2.8 MB].

How to join our Netflix Watch Party

Netflix Party is a way to fun watch Coded Bias (90 minutes) online with your fellow delegates as it adds group chat to Netflix. Delegates will be sent a link to join the Netflix Party.

Step 1: Register using the form

Register for our Coded Bias Netflix Watch Party using this form. Netflix Watch Parties are restricted to 50 people.

Step 2: Install Teleparty

  1. Ensure you have Google Chrome installed on your computer or laptop.
  2. Open Chrome, go to this page, and click the Install Teleparty button.
  3. Once you are redirected to the Chrome Web Store, click Add to Chrome to finish installing Teleparty.

Step 3: Pin Teleparty to Chrome Toolbar

After installing Teleparty, you should see the Tp button next to your address bar.

If you don’t see the NP button, click the puzzle icon next to the address bar and pin the Tp button to your Chrome toolbar.

Step 4: Join our Coded Bias Teleparty

At 19:50 (London) on Friday 21 May, we will send you the URL to join our Netflix Watch Party, which will start at 20:00.
