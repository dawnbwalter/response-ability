Independent FinTech analyst

Dr Anette Broløs is an independent FinTech analyst and experienced network leader working with strategic innovation and partnerships. Anette holds an industrial PhD in collaborative innovation and has a background in economics (Master and MBA). Anette has extensive work experience in finance including six years as CEO in Copenhagen FinTech Innovation and Research. Together with Dr Erin B. Taylor, she is an EWPN Research Lead.
