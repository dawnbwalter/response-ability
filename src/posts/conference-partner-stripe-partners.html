We are delighted to announce Stripe Partners, a London-based strategy and innovation consultancy, as a Conference Partner.

Simon Roberts, Partner and Co-founder, and also keynote at the conference, explains their reason for supporting the Anthropology + Technology Conference 2019:

“Stripe Partners is proud to be supporting the Anthropology + Technology Conference in its inaugural year. Deepening the dialogue between social scientists and technologists is critical if we are to ensure that society benefits from AI that embodies the different disciplinary perspectives and insights on offer across academia and industry. Getting people in the same room to share, learn, discuss and debate is a vital first step and we’re delighted to be helping that conversation flourish.”

Thanks so much, Stripe Partners, we really value your support! In the meantime, check out Stripe Partners’ website and their writing.
