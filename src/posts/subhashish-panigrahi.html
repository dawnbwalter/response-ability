Documentary filmmaker and researcher

Subhashish is a documentary filmmaker, researcher, and Openness advocate. He was a Yoti Digital Identity Fellow in 2019 and has led programme design, strategy, communications and community-catalyst roles in not-for-profit organisations including Wikimedia Foundation, Centre for Internet & Society, Mozilla, and Internet Society.

Subhashish has produced several documentary films focusing on the protection of indigenous and endangered languages including three films for National Geographic under the aegis of OpenSpeaks, a project that he founded in 2017.
