Diversity Sponsor

We’re looking for a sponsor for our PechaKucha (lightning talks) session. Your sponsorship will enable us to offer financial support — such as travel and accommodation costs, a free ticket, and/or money to cover loss of income through taking time off work — to those who need it.

We’re asking for help because of conversations with people from underrepresented groups who want to attend and give a talk but can’t unless we help them with costs. Which we can’t without sponsorship. Which is frustrating for us and them.

If you want to help sponsor people from underrepresented groups to attend our conference and give a PechaKucha talk and who need financial support so that we can bring diverse perspectives to the important topic of socially-responsible AI, please get in touch.
